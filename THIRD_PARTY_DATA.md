| Indicator | Unit | Source and license statement |
|---|---|---|
| GDP per capita (`NY.GDP.PCAP.CD`) | current US$ | [World Bank indicator](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/NY.GDP.PCAP.CD) |
| Consumer price inflation (`FP.CPI.TOTL.ZG`) | annual % | [World Bank indicator](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/FP.CPI.TOTL.ZG) |
| Population (`SP.POP.TOTL`) | persons | [World Bank indicator](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.POP.TOTL) |

Underlying credits: CPI/inflation — IMF International Financial Statistics; GDP — national statistical offices/central banks, OECD and World Bank staff estimates; population — UN Population Division, national statistical offices, Eurostat and UN Statistics Division as listed on the indicator page.

Exact acquisition URLs, UTC retrieval times and SHA-256 checksums appear in `data/source_manifest.json`. The snapshot uses the latest historical vintage acquired for this project, not a real-time vintage archive. Raw JSON is retained. Transformations select countries/years, reshape observations and produce analytical CSVs/figures; the original published values are not fabricated or imputed. Code and third-party software licenses are separate from these dataset terms.
